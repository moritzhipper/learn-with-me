What is {{ appName }}?

This app helps you create, practice, and organize vocabulary and phrase cards, while keeping track of your confidence so you can see your progress.

You can pull cards from any articles, notes, random snippets of any language you know or are learning. Use it to prepare for real life moments like ordering food or chatting about your favorite topics. Or dont and create your vocab cards by hand. :)

How to use

Once you've created some vocab cards, you can jump into practice. For each guessing direction, the app tracks your last 5 correct and incorrect guesses, turning them into confidence points shown as little dots on each card. Guess correctly 5 times in every direction to reach 100% confidence for that card.

Group your cards into collections by topic, skill, or whatever makes sense for you. Check the average confidence score for each collection to quickly see which areas need more practice.

Import / Export

Want to share or save your cards in a third place? You can export a single collection from its collection view, or export all your collections at once from the settings. When you're ready to bring them back, just import them via the Collections page.
